If you are reading this weeknote around the day it was posted, your looking at
my brand new re-designed website. Some of the goals the previous version tried
to achieve were solved. Mainly, employment, haha. The linear blog only wished me
to post technical, polished and finished articles. Now, the plan is non-linear.
A wiki that evolves with me. The few bits of linearity you will read are these
weeknotes.

I wanted the design to be truly mine. I even designed the typeface you are
reading these words in myself. Though the design is my product, the infrastructure
was result of an experiment. I've been trying to make more use of LLMs at work.
My opinion on them has kept on getting positive, overcoming the fear of them drowing
me in their flattery and bullshit. I'm still uncertain about their effects on my
mental health and ability to gain skills, but them being not fun is no longer the case.

I've started creative coding again, by hand, my retreat from the all-knowing
oracles. I wrote a few demo effects, I took a bit of help from the clankers with
math, but I feel I've learned from the exercise.

I am invited to talk about the demoscene to an upcoming creative coding event.
Initially, I was a bit concerned with me not doing justice and misguiding people
as a result, but a poor talk is better than no talk.

One activist from my home city has gotten popular for his rich language.
I really enjoyed listening to my native language in such richness and it reminds
me of how much beautiful and complex the languages we speak are. I'm disappointed that
when I was younger, I did not appreciate language as much as I do now, part of it
is fault of society and pop culture that made me treat education, math and literature
as something that isn't for everyone. Or another explanation could be that my appreciation
for syntax of programming languages affected it.

I'm writing this weeknote at a Indie Web Club BLR session. While coming here,
I noticed how our roads are full of advertisements obstructing the pretty skies.
Not sure if vandalism like the graffiti artists do is a good solution,
but that does come with the advantage of some beautiful art.
