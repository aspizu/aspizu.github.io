Your're here because I somehow convinced you to read more about the Demoscene.

I keep talking about the Demoscene, but what is it exactly?

I learned programming from Scratch, look at the [3D Graphics and Scratch](https://scratch.mit.edu/studios/32030084/) community
for examples of what people (most of them are very young) do on Scratch.
Around the same time I started programming, I also discovered the [Demoscene](https://demoscene-the-art-of-coding.net/).

Before I got into programming, I was learning to produce electronic music.
Demos involve all of the three art forms: code, music, graphics

Each stimulate the senses computers currently have the capability to: code for
the brain, music for the ears, graphics for the eyes.

Creating a demo requires excellence in all three crafts.

Demos need limitations, demos beat limitations. Old computers are a fitting choice.
They are also simpler in design, simplicity and the constraints thereof are beautiful.

I believe AI has no place in the demoscene, demos are not useful software, we don't
NEED demos, its an art form.

Demoscene productions I've made can be found at my [Demozoo](https://demozoo.org/sceners/125624/) profile.
